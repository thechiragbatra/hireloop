# Privacy Policy

- **In-memory processing:** Resumes are processed in memory during the session.
- **TTL deletion:** Data is deleted after session TTL (default 3600 seconds).
- **No logging:** Resume text and profile content are never logged. Only IDs, sizes, and timings are logged.
- **No scraping:** We do not scrape LinkedIn, Naukri, or Indeed.
- **Bring your own key:** You can provide your own LLM API key. Shared keys are rate-limited by IP.
- **No auto-submit:** The app never auto-submits job applications.
